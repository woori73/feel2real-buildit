1서답형 없음 = 객관식 스피드전. "있는 대로 고르기(2개)"·"개수 세기"는 하나씩 ○✕ 마킹 후 답. 감으로 ⑤ 고르면 무조건 틀린다.
2so는 형용사/부사 바로 앞. She studied so hard that ○ / studied hard so that ✕ — 직전 학습지에서 반이 진짜로 틀렸다.
3전환하면 주체·목적어 점검. too~to 주어가 다르면 for us(too loud to sleep ✕). can't 있던 문장을 enough to로 바꾸면 의미 뒤집힘.
4"was died"는 오답 선택지로만 존재. die 자동사 → died. 안장 → was buried in Seoul National Cemetery. 오답 모의고사 27번 실측.
5지각동사 뒤 p.p.는 미끼. saw/hear/feel + O + 원형·-ing만 정답. sung·beaten·to shout 보이면 즉시 삭제.
6continue = to V / 전치사 = -ing. continued to write · famous for using · without leaving. "continue write ✕, for to use ✕".
7historic vs historical — 이번 주 출제 예고. 역사적으로 중요한 = historic(a historic event). 역사 관련 = historical(historical novel).
8used to 오염 3형제. She uses to ✕ → used to / used to having ✕ → have / There are used to be ✕ → are 삭제. "익숙하다"는 be used to + -ing만.

5과 11 — 정의 순환 함정 주의

artwork — a painting/drawing/sculpture by an artist

dot — a very small round mark (점)
figure — a person/animal in a painting (인물)
hip — following the latest fashions (세련된)
leafless — having no leaves
lively — full of life and energy
modern — of the present/recent times
opinion — a thought or belief about something
pleasure — a state of feeling happy

6과 11 — Schofield 지문과 세트로 회수

article — a piece of writing in a newspaper/magazine
bury — to put into a hole and cover it (p.p. buried)
cemetery — a place where dead bodies are put
continue — to keep doing without stopping (to V/-ing)
foreign(er) — from another country / a person ~
gather — to come together in a group
government — the group that controls a country
hide — to go where you will not be seen (was hiding)
historic — important in history (≠ historical)
independence — freedom from political control
invitation — a request to come to an event

혼동 최종선별 5 — "예외 없이 이대로"

  • historic(역사적 중요) vs historical(역사 관련) — 선생님이 "역사적으로 중요한"에 historic 직접 확인
  • recommend vs research vs remember — 권하다/조사하다/기억하다
  • foreign(외국의) vs foreigner(외국인) — "a foreigner who loved Korea"
  • independence(독립, 名) vs independent(독립된, 形)
  • bury(행위, 타동사→was buried) vs die→died (was died ✕)
