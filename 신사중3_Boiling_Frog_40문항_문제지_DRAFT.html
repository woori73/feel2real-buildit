11.다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은? (3점)
The story of the boiling frog is a myth: a real frog can detect slowly rising temperatures and escape. Still, the story remains a powerful metaphor because humans often ignore changes that happen gradually. Climate change is a good example. Since 1850, the Earth's global average temperature has risen by about 1°C. That may not sound significant, but an average can hide much larger changes in particular places. Humans are good at adapting to gradual changes, so we may become oblivious to changes that once seemed unusual. Small changes can accumulate over time, and their consequences can eventually become impossible to ignore.
① A real frog can notice slowly rising temperatures and escape.
② Humans often do not pay attention to changes that happen gradually.
③ The Earth's global average temperature has risen by about 1°C since 1850.
④ An average can hide much larger changes in particular places.
⑤ Small changes disappear before they can cause any problems.
