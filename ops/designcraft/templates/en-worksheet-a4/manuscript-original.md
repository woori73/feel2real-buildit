## 지문 원문 (Original Passage)

The rapid development of artificial intelligence has transformed the way we live and work. While some fear that machines will replace human workers, others argue that AI will create new opportunities that we cannot yet imagine. History suggests that technological revolutions, from the steam engine to the internet, have ultimately expanded human possibilities rather than diminished them.

What matters now is not whether AI will change our lives — it already has — but how we choose to adapt. Education systems must evolve to cultivate creativity and critical thinking, qualities that remain uniquely human. Those who embrace lifelong learning will thrive in the age of intelligent machines.

## 빈칸 채우기

1. The rapid __________ of artificial intelligence has transformed the way we live and work.

2. History suggests that technological revolutions have ultimately __________ human possibilities.

3. Education systems must evolve to __________ creativity and critical thinking.
