11.다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은? (3점)
The story of the boiling frog is a myth: a real frog can detect slowly rising temperatures and escape. Still, the story remains a powerful metaphor because humans often ignore changes that happen gradually. Climate change is a good example. Since 1850, the Earth's global average temperature has risen by about 1°C. That may not sound significant, but an average can hide much larger changes in particular places. Humans are good at adapting to gradual changes, so we may become oblivious to changes that once seemed unusual. Small changes can accumulate over time, and their consequences can eventually become impossible to ignore.
① A real frog can notice slowly rising temperatures and escape.
② Humans often do not pay attention to changes that happen gradually.
③ The Earth's global average temperature has risen by about 1°C since 1850.
④ An average can hide much larger changes in particular places.
⑤ Small changes disappear before they can cause any problems.

16.다음 글을 읽고 추론할 수 없는 것은? (3점)
Scientists have identified 1.5°C of warming above pre-industrial levels as an important threshold. Beyond this point, many negative effects of climate change are expected to become increasingly severe. More extreme heat, rising sea levels, and damage to ecosystems could become harder to mitigate. One important goal is net zero: reducing greenhouse gas emissions to the point where the amount added to the atmosphere is balanced by the amount removed. Individuals can also reduce their carbon footprints by using less energy, taking public transportation, and making more sustainable choices. In some cases, we can offset emissions or remove carbon from the atmosphere.
① Scientists see 1.5°C of warming as an important threshold.
② Negative effects of climate change are expected to become more severe beyond 1.5°C.
③ Net zero means balancing the greenhouse gases added to the atmosphere with the amount removed.
④ Individuals can help by using less energy and taking public transportation.
⑤ Governments have already reached net zero in most countries.
17.다음 글의 제목으로 가장 적절한 것은? (3점)
The story of the boiling frog is not scientifically true, but it teaches us something important. Humans often fail to notice slow changes, just like the frog in warm water. Climate change works the same way: small changes accumulate over time, and their consequences become impossible to ignore. The risks are serious, but we can still recognize the problem and change our actions before it becomes too serious.
