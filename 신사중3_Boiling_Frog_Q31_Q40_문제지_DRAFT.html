31.다음은 비디오 이해 질문과 그에 대한 학생의 답이다. 답의 근거가 되는 본문 문장으로 가장 적절한 것은? (3점)
[비디오 이해 질문] According to the video, why does burning fossil fuels contribute to global warming?
[학생의 답] Because burning fossil fuels releases gases that trap heat.
① Much of modern life depends on fossil fuels, such as coal, oil, and natural gas.
② When we burn them, we release carbon dioxide, an important greenhouse gas, into the atmosphere.
③ These gases trap heat that would otherwise escape into space.
④ Our everyday choices also contribute to this problem through our carbon footprint.
⑤ If we continue at the current rate, these activities could exacerbate climate change.

Climate change is a good example. Since 1850, the Earth's global average temperature has risen by about 1°C. ⓐ That may not sound significant, but an average can hide much larger changes in particular places. ⓑ Humans are good at adapting to gradual changes, so we may become oblivious to changes that once seemed unusual. ⓒ Small changes can accumulate over time, and their consequences can eventually become impossible to ignore. ⓓ This makes climate change an insidious problem. ⓔ

33.다음 글의 내용을 한 문장으로 요약하고자 한다. 빈칸 (A), (B)에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 것은? (3점)
The boiling frog story is a myth, but it works as a powerful metaphor. Humans often fail to notice gradual changes, and climate change is a good example. Small changes accumulate over time, and their consequences become impossible to ignore. The risks are serious, but we can still take action before it is too late.

③ (A) gradual (B) escape
④ (A) small (B) doubt
⑤ (A) sudden (B) adapt

That may not sound significant, but an average can hide much larger changes in particular places. Scientists have identified 1.5°C of warming above pre-industrial levels as an important threshold. 36.다음은 학교 학습자료에 나온 myth의 정의이다. 이 정의에 해당하는 사례를 모두 고르면? (정답 2개, 4점)
Myth: A story that is believed or told as if it were true, but may not be scientifically accurate.
① "Goldfish have a three-second memory." — 과학적으로 사실이 아님에도 널리 퍼진 이야기
② "Water freezes at 0°C." — 과학적으로 검증된 사실을 설명한 문장
③ "Bats can see using echolocation." — 실제로 과학적으로 검증된 사실
④ "The Earth orbits the Sun." — 천문학적으로 검증된 사실
⑤ "Lightning never strikes the same place twice." — 사실이 아니지만 널리 퍼진 믿음
