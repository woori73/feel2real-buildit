Hi, I’m Bella. Last Friday, I saw my brother playing basketball after school. He was playing really well, so I took a picture of him. My brother is good at math and science. Then I shared the picture on my social media. Over the weekend, something went wrong. Someone made a copy of the picture with bad comments. The copy was spread here and there, and I was so embarrassed. My brother wanted me to delete the picture of him. I apologized to him, and I deleted my post immediately. However, the copies still remain online.

27.다음 문장과 같은 의미인 것은?
Someone saw him enter the building.
① He was seen enter the building.② He was seen to enter the building.③ He was saw to enter the building.④ He is seen to enter the building.⑤ He has seen to enter the building.
28.대화의 내용과 일치하는 것은?
A: Did you see my new photo? I shared it yesterday.
B: Yes! I left a comment on it. It was really nice.
A: Thanks! But someone I don’t know sent me a strange message.
B: Really? You should not answer unknown messages.
A: You’re right. I deleted it immediately and told my mom about it.
B: Good. If you see it again, please contact the website.
① B uploaded a new photo yesterday.② A answered the strange message.③ A will keep the strange message.④ B left a comment on A’s photo.⑤ B told A to share more photos.
29.글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?
