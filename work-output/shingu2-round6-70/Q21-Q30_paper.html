23.주어진 단어의 영영 풀이를 활용한 예문이 적절하지 않은 것은?
  1. ① bury: to put something into the ground and cover it / They buried the time capsule under the tree.
  2. ② government: the group of people who officially control a country / The government decided to build a new hospital.
  3. ③ recommend: to suggest that someone or something would be good / Which dish do you recommend for lunch?
  4. ④ invitation: a written request for asking someone to come to an event / He ate an invitation for dinner.
  5. ⑤ research: to collect information about something / They researched many restaurants before they ate out.

29.다음 단어의 영영 풀이와 예문의 연결이 옳지 않은 것은?
  1. ① continue: to keep doing something without stopping / They continued to play soccer in the rain.
  2. ② historic: important in history / Rome is famous for its delicious buildings.
  3. ③ hospital: a place where sick people stay / He went to the hospital yesterday.
  4. ④ novel: a long story about imaginary characters and events / She enjoys reading historical novels.
  5. ⑤ volunteer: to do a job without being paid for it / He plans to volunteer at the hospital.
