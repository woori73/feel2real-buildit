[공통대화] Sebit Park (Q3)

B: Somi, I need help. What’s the best way to Sebit Park from here?
G: Sebit Park? Well, you can get there by bus or by subway, but the subway is better.
B: OK. Which line should I take? G: Take line 3, and get off at Sebit Station.
B: Line 3 and get off at Sebit Station. How long will it take?
G: It’ll take about 40 minutes. B: Is it that far?
G: Yes. Anyway, why are you going to Sebit Park, Liam?
B: Oh, there is an exhibition this Saturday. You can come with me if you want.
G: Really? I’m free this Saturday. Let’s go together.
B: Perfect. Let’s meet at 10.

[Reading] 다음 글을 읽고 물음에 답하시오. (Q5~Q8, Street Art)

Street Art is changing our city. STIK paints simple figures with lines and dots on old walls in Shoreditch. One of the hippest areas in London, Shoreditch used to be quiet, but now many people visit it to see colorful murals. Banksy sprays green paint on a wall in Finsbury Park, and the wall looks like a real tree. Ben Wilson paints tiny pictures on gum that people drop on the street. His small paintings give great pleasure to many citizens.
