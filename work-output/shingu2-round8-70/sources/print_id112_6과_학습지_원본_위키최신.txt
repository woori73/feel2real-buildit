\r\nso+형용사/부사+that+주어+can't[couldn't]+동사원형 → too+형용사/부사+to+동사원형
\r\nso+형용사/부사+that+주어+can[could]+동사원형 → 형용사/부사+enough to+동사원형
\r\n\r\nI’m so tired that I can’t play soccer with you.
\r\n→ I’m ( ) tired ( ) play soccer with you.
\r\nThe water was so clear that we could see the fish.
\r\n→ The water was ( ) see the fish.
