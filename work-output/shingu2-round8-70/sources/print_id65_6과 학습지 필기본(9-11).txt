Grade 2　어법 2. so ~ that | 이름: ______ | 
[어법 예문]
The movie is so boring that I want to cry.
Last night, Nick was so tired that he went to bed early.
The book is so difficult that children can't understand it.
[어법 설명]「so+형용사/부사+that...」은 ‘너무[매우] ~해서 …하다’의 뜻으로, 어떤 원인 때문에 발생한 결과를 나타낼 때 사용한다.
so+형용사/부사+that+주어+can't[couldn't]+동사원형 → too+형용사/부사+to+동사원형
so+형용사/부사+that+주어+can[could]+동사원형 → 형용사/부사+enough to+동사원형
I’m so tired that I can’t play soccer with you.
→ I’m ( too ) tired ( to ) play soccer with you.
The water was so clear that we could see the fish.
→ The water was ( clear enough to ) see the fish.
A　다음 괄호 안에 주어진 단어를 바르게 배열하여 문장을 완성해 봅시다.
The orange was so delicious that I had three more. (delicious, that, so)
This computer is so small that I can carry it. (so, that, small)
I was so busy that I couldn't answer the phone. (busy, that, so)
You are so young that you can't see the movie. (young, so, that)
The weather is so beautiful that I can't stay at home. (so, that, beautiful)
John was so tired that he couldn't do his homework. (tired, that, so)
B　다음 우리말과 뜻이 같도록 주어진 단어를 사용하여 영작해 봅시다.
나는 너무 피곤해서 어젯밤에 일찍 잠자리에 들었다. I was so tired that I went to bed early yesterday.
그들은 너무 더워서 더 이상 일할 수가 없었다. They were so hot that they couldn't work any longer.
파도가 매우 높아서 우리는 바다에서 수영할 수 없다. The waves are so high that we can't swim in the ocean.
그 상자는 너무 무거워서 내가 그것을 옮길 수 없었다. The box was so heavy that I couldn't carry it.
그녀는 매우 열심히 공부해서 그 시험에 합격했다. She studied hard so that she passed the exam.
 ← 오답인 것 같음: so ~ that 구문을 사용해야 함. 정답: She studied so hard that she passed the exam.
C　다음 문장을 “too ... to ...”, “enough to ...” 형태로 바꾸세요.
The box is so heavy that I can't lift it. → The box is too heavy to lift it.
The homework is so difficult that he can't finish it. → The homework is difficult enough to finish it.
The coffee was so hot that I couldn't drink it. → The coffee was too hot to drink.
The noise was so loud that we couldn't sleep. → The noise was too loud to sleep.
 ← 오답인 것 같음 · 정답: The noise was too loud for us to sleep.
She was so tired that she couldn't continue working. → She was too tired to continue working.
The cake was so delicious that I could eat the whole thing. → The cake was delicious enough to eat the whole thing.
The car is so fast that it can win the race. → The car is fast enough to win the race.

English 2　Grammar1 지각동사 | 이름 ______ | 
지각동사: 감각을 나타내는 동사(목적보어 자리에 동사원형을 취하는 특이한 동사)
보다seelook atwatch
듣다hearlisten to느끼다feel
지각동사 + 목적어 + 동사원형 or ~ing (5형식)
큰 의미 차이는 없지만, ~ing를 쓰면 진행의 의미가 좀 더 강조된다.
e.g. I saw my brother [ do / doing / to do ] his homework.
그림을 보고, see 또는 hear와 괄호 안의 표현을 사용해 Andy가 보거나 들은 것을 예시와 같이 쓰시오.
e.g. Andy heard Julie call(ing) her dog's name.
Andy heard[saw] Lisa playing the guitar. (the guitar)
Andy saw Suho swimming in the sea. (in the sea)
Andy heard Sam talking on the phone. (on the phone)
Andy saw Hana building a sandcastle. (a sandcastle)
4. 괄호 안에 알맞은 형태의 단어를 고르시오.
I saw ( they / them ) swim in the lake.
She heard the doorbell ( ring / to ring ).
Did you see ( he / him ) climbing up the tree?
We felt the ground ( shaking / to shake ) for a few seconds.
5. 다음 문장을 주어진 단어로 시작하는 문장으로 바꿔 써 봅시다.
The dog was coming to me. → I saw the dog coming to me.
My heart was beating fast. → I felt my heart beating fast.
She was crying in the next room. → I heard her crying in the next room.
6. 괄호 안의 표현을 활용하여 우리말을 영어로 쓰시오.
(she, pull, my hair) → I could feel her pulling my hair.
(his poems, recite, the poet) → We were listening to the poet reciting his poems.
(for the first time, walk, their baby) → The parents were watching their baby walking for the first time.
(shine, the stars, brightly) → We enjoy looking at the stars shining brightly in the night sky.
