Reading A
Street Art in London
Modern street art comes in many different forms, and it makes cities colorful and lively. Today, we are going to see some well-known street artworks in London. The place that we are going to visit is Shoreditch. Poor workers used to live in this area, but now it is one of the hippest areas in London. We can find so many great pieces of street art here. Do you see the painting over there? It is by the artist STIK. He is famous for using only lines and dots in his paintings of human bodies. According to STIK, the three figures represent the past, present, and future of Shoreditch. Which figure represents which? Well, look closely at the eyes of the three figures. They are just dots, but they will give you a clue.
11.다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?
①STIK relies only on lines and dots to depict the human body in his paintings.
②Shoreditch is a place they are not going to visit today.
③Poor workers currently live in Shoreditch, an unpopular area.
④The three figures in STIK's painting represent three different cities.
⑤Visitors are told not to look closely at the painting.
12.다음 문장에서 밑줄 친 부분이 의미하는 것으로 가장 적절한 것은?

Reading B
Banksy & Finsbury Park
We are now in Finsbury Park to see a painting by Banksy, an internationally famous street artist. Banksy's artworks often carry social messages, and the green tree you see over there is a good example. As you can see, he created a green tree by spraying green paint on a wall behind a leafless tree. Next to the tree, there is a person who is holding a sprayer. What message does Banksy want to give us through this painting? In many people's opinion, he is telling us that nature is having a hard time. They think he wants us to take better care of it.
13.다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

③Most people think Banksy's tree painting has nothing to do with nature.
④Banksy created the green tree by spraying paint on a wall behind a leafless tree.
⑤People think Banksy wants viewers to take better care of nature.

Reading C
Ben Wilson & Muswell Hill
The final stop of our tour today is Muswell Hill. Can't see any street artworks? Well, look down and look closely. Do you see the little chewing gum paintings? They are by the artist Ben Wilson. Wilson is well known for painting pictures on gum that people drop on the street. His gum paintings often carry personal messages from people he meets on the street. For example, the one over here is a happy birthday wish for a loved one. Wilson's gum paintings are tiny, but they give great pleasure to many people.
15.다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?
①The final stop of the tour is Shoreditch.
②Visitors are told to look up to find the artworks.
③Ben Wilson creates his artworks on gum that people have thrown away on the street.
④Wilson's gum paintings are large murals covering entire walls.
⑤Wilson's paintings never carry any personal messages.
