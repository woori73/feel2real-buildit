 Salesperson: Can I help you?
 Boy: Yes. I'm looking for a gift for my younger sister. She's twelve.
 Salesperson: How about this small bag?
 Boy: Well, ① can you show me something else?
 Salesperson: How about these hairpins, then?
 Boy: Oh, they're so pretty! How much are they?
 Salesperson: They are 10 dollars.
 Boy: That's reasonable. I'll take them. By the way, is there any discount?
 Salesperson: I'm afraid not. That is already a discounted price.
 Boy: Okay. Here is my card.

 [문항 16] (배점 4점)
 주어진 단어의 영영 풀이를 활용한 예문이 적절하지 않은 것은? (4점)
 allowance: money that you are given regularly
 → My parents increase my allowance if I clean my room.
 budget: the amount of money you can spend
 → We need to stay within our budget for the school festival.
 discount: a lower price than usual
 → The store offered a 20% discount on all items.
 strategy: a detailed plan for achieving something or reaching a goal
 → The team came up with a new strategy to win the game.
 anchor: a suggestion that someone gives you about what you should do in a particular situation
 → The captain dropped the anchor to keep the ship from moving.
