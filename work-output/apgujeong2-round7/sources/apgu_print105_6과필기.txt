[2026 압구정중학교 2학년 영어] Lesson 6 Grammar 1 [see/hear/feel+목적어+-ing]

Part 1. 개념정리

• 지각동사 : 감각 기관을 통해 대상을 인식하는 의미를 나타내는 동사로 see(보다), hear(듣다), feel(느끼다), watch(지켜보다), smell(냄새 맡다) 등이 있다.

• see/hear/feel+목적어+-ing: 「주어+동사+목적어+목적격 보어」로 이루어진 5형식 문장에서 동사가 see, hear, feel 등의 지각동사일 경우 목적격 보어로 -ing 형태의 현재분사가 오며 ‘(목적어)가 ~하는 것을 보다/듣다/느끼다’라는 뜻을 나타낸다.

e.g. He saw me climbing the tree. (그는 내가 나무를 올라가고 있는 것을 보았다.)

She heard the man playing the piano. (그녀는 그 남자가 피아노를 치고 있는 소리를 들었다.)

I felt my body trembling. (나는 내 몸이 떨리는 것을 느꼈다.)

• 지각동사의 목적격 보어: 5형식 문장에서 지각동사(see, hear, feel 등)는 목적격 보어로 현재분사(-ing) 뿐만 아니라 의미에 따라 동사원형 또는 과거분사를 취할 수 있다.

(1) 현재분사(-ing): 목적어의 동작이 진행 중임을 강조하여 부분적인 동작이나 상황을 설명한다.

e.g. She heard the baby crying. (그녀는 아기가 울고 있는 소리를 들었다.)

(2) 동사원형: 목적어의 동작이나 행위 전체를 설명한다.

e.g. I saw him fly a kite. (나는 그가 연 날리는 것을 보았다.)

(3) 과거분사(-ed): 목적어와 목적격 보어가 수동의 관계로 동작이 완료된 상태를 설명한다.

e.g. I heard my name called. (나는 내 이름이 불리는 것을 들었다.)

수업 필기: bake → baked / bread baked = 수동 관계. cook · brew · close · open 등도 목적어와의 능동/수동 관계를 확인.

Part 2. 개념 확인 문제

A. 빈 칸을 채워봅시다.

1) He saw the boys playing soccer. (그는 소년들이 축구하는 것을 보았다.)

2) I heard her laughing. (나는 그녀가 웃는 것을 들었다.)

3) She felt the wind blowing. (그녀는 바람이 부는 것을 느꼈다.)

4) I smelled the bread baking. (나는 빵 굽는 냄새를 맡았다.)

5) I heard the baby crying. (나는 아기가 우는 것을 들었다.)

B. 괄호 안의 단어를 알맞은 것으로 고르시오.

1. I saw him (cross / to cross) the street alone. → cross

2. Did you hear the phone (ring / rung) a moment ago? → ring

3. I felt someone (touch / touched) my shoulder in the dark. → touch

4. We watched the sun (rise / risen) over the sea. → rise

5. She noticed a stranger (following / to follow) her. → following

6. I heard my name (call / called) from behind. → called

7. Have you ever seen a building (destroy / destroyed) by a fire? → destroyed

8. I smelled something (burn / burning) in the kitchen. → burning

9. I watched the birds (fed / feeding) their babies. → feeding

10. Did you feel the house (shake / shaken) during the earthquake? → shake

11. I saw a little girl (to cry / crying) in the park. → crying

12. I heard him (play / played) the piano in the next room. → play

13. We saw the car (steal / stolen) by a thief. → stolen

14. Did you see the cat (jump / to jump) onto the roof? → jump

15. I watched the old man (paint / painted) the wall. → paint

Lesson 6 Grammar 1 · Part 3. 심화 문제

A. 다음 문장에서 어법상 어색한 부분을 찾아 바르게 고쳐 쓰시오. (이상 없으면 ‘이상 없음’)

1. I saw the window to be broken by a stone. → I saw the window being broken by a stone.

2. Did you hear him sang a song at the party? → Did you hear him sing a song at the party?

3. She felt herself being followed by someone. → 이상 없음

4. I heard my mother mentioned my name to her friend. → I heard my mother mention my name to her friend.

B. 주어진 우리말 뜻과 일치하도록 영작하시오.

1. 나는 내 방이 누군가에 의해 청소되는 것을 보았다. (see, clean)
→ I saw my room being cleaned by someone.

2. 너는 그 아기가 자고 있는 것을 본 적이 있니? (ever see, sleep)
→ Have you ever seen the baby sleeping?

3. 그는 그의 심장이 점점 더 빨리 뛰는 것을 느꼈다. (feel, beat)
→ He felt his heart beating faster and faster.

4. 우리는 지도가 바람에 날아가는 것을 지켜보았다. (watch, blow away)
→ We watched the map blown away by the wind.

5. 나는 누군가가 밖에서 내 이름을 부르는 소리를 들었다. (hear, call)
→ I heard someone calling my name outside.

6. 그녀는 부엌에서 맛있는 냄새가 나고 있는 것을 맡았다. (smell, cook)
→ She smelled something delicious cooking in the kitchen.

7. 당신은 누군가 당신을 쳐다보고 있다는 것을 느꼈나요? (feel, stare at)
→ Did you feel someone staring at you?

C. 문장 변형 및 응용

☆ 1. Someone saw him enter the building. (He로 시작하는 수동태로)
→ He was seen to enter the building.

☆ 2. I heard her crying in her room. (She로 시작하는 수동태로)
→ She was heard crying in her room.

3. (smelled / the / I / coffee / being / brewed.) → I smelled the coffee being brewed.

☆ 4. I saw a boy cross the street alone. (A boy로 시작하는 수동태로)
→ A boy was seen to cross the street alone.

☆ 5. We heard someone knock on the door. (Someone으로 시작하는 수동태로)
→ Someone was heard to knock on the door.

6. I saw him paint (paint) the door. (‘전 과정’ 강조)

7. I saw him painting (paint) the door. (‘진행 중인 순간’ 강조)

8. I saw the door closing (close) (문이 닫히는 것)

9. I saw the thief (catch / caught) by the police. → caught

10. I felt the cold wind (blow / blown) through the window. → blow

11. I heard the engine (start / starting). → start

[2026 압구정중학교 2학년 영어] Lesson 6 Grammar 2 [want/tell/ask+목적어+to부정사]

Part 1. 개념정리

• 「주어+동사+목적어+목적격 보어」로 이루어진 5형식 문장에서 want, tell, ask 등의 동사가 사용될 때 목적격 보어로 to부정사를 취하여 「주어+want/tell/ask+목적어+to부정사」 형태로 쓴다. 이때 목적격 보어는 주어가 목적어에게 바라는 행동을 나타낸다.

(1) want+목적어+to부정사: (목적어)가 ~하기를 원하다

e.g. I want you to study harder. (나는 네가 더 열심히 공부하기를 원한다.)

They want her to join the club. (그들은 그녀가 동아리에 가입하기를 원한다.)

(2) tell+목적어+to부정사: (목적어)에게 ~하라고 말하다

e.g. The teacher told us to open the book. (선생님은 우리에게 책을 펴라고 말씀하셨다.)

Mom told me to clean my room. (엄마는 나에게 방을 청소하라고 말씀하셨다.)

(3) ask+목적어+to부정사: (목적어)에게 ~해 달라고 요청하다

e.g. Can I ask you to help me? (내가 너에게 날 도와달라고 요청해도 될까?)

They asked us to wait outside. (그들은 우리에게 밖에서 기다려 달라고 요청했다.)

수업 필기: expect, advise, order, allow, encourage, warn 등도 목적어 뒤에 to부정사를 목적격 보어로 취하는 5형식 동사로 함께 정리. 부정은 not to + 동사원형.

Part 2. 개념 확인 문제

A. 빈칸을 채워봅시다.

1) I want him to leave early. (나는 그가 일찍 떠나기를 원한다.)

2) She told me to wait here. (그녀는 내게 여기서 기다리라고 말했다.)

3) He ordered his dog to sit down. (그는 그의 개에게 앉으라고 명령했다.)

4) He asked her to cook something for him. (그는 그녀에게 무언가를 요리해 달라고 요청했다.)

5) Do you want them to help you? (너는 그들이 너를 도와주기를 원하니?)

B. 괄호 안에서 어법상 알맞은 것을 고르시오.

1. My mom wanted me (clean / to clean) my bedroom. → to clean

2. The teacher told us (not to talk / don't talk) during the exam. → not to talk

3. I asked him (to help / help) me with my homework. → to help

4. She expects her son (pass / to pass) the entrance exam. → to pass

5. My doctor advised me (to drink / drinking) more water every day. → to drink

6. The policeman ordered the driver (to stop / stop) the car. → to stop

7. My father allowed me (go / to go) to the concert with my friends. → to go

8. She asked her brother (not to play / to not play) loud music at night. → not to play

9. The teacher wants all students (to turn / turn) in their homework on time. → to turn

10. He told his daughter (to wash / washing) her hands before dinner. → to wash

11. We expect him (be / to be) on time for the meeting. → to be

12. The counselor advised us (not to make / to not make) hasty decisions. → not to make

13. My friend encouraged me (to try / trying) out for the school play. → to try

14. The museum guard warned people (not to touch / to not touch) the statues. → not to touch

15. She asked me (lend / to lend) her my umbrella. → to lend

16. I told the driver (drive / to drive) more carefully. → to drive

17. They want us (join / to join) their science club. → to join

Lesson 6 Grammar 2 · Part 3. 심화 문제

A. 다음 문장에서 어법상 어색한 부분을 찾아 바르게 고쳐 쓰시오. (이상 없으면 ‘이상 없음’)

→ She told me not to touch anything in her room.

→ The coach encouraged us to work hard for the upcoming tournament.

4) My dad allowed me to use his new computer.
→ 이상 없음

5) He asked me to not tell anyone his secret.
→ He asked me not to tell anyone his secret.

→ My mom told me to start studying for the exam.

→ He asked me not to touch his camera.

→ The teacher wants us to help each other.

→ We expect him to win the game.

→ My teacher told me not to be late.

2) I hope that you will come to my birthday party.
→ I want you to come to my birthday party.

3) “Could you please turn down the volume?” + She said to me.
→ She asked me to turn down the volume.
