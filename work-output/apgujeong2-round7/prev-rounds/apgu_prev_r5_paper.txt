15. 다음 글의 내용과 일치하지 않는 것은?

Well, I've always had a passion for sports and writing. Writing about sports makes me excited. There was a big baseball game yesterday. How did you spend the day? I arrived at the baseball park early. Before the game started, I had an interview with the players. I took a lot of notes during the game. After the game, I went to the press conference with other journalists. I asked the players how they felt about the game. In the afternoon, I had to write an article and edit it very quickly. My article was posted online. I love writing about sports.
① Ms. Ali has a passion for sports and writing.
② She arrived at the baseball park early.
③ She interviewed the players before the game started.
④ She wrote and edited her article slowly after the game.
⑤ Her article was posted online.

17. 다음 글의 내용과 일치하는 것은?
I want to know why you wanted to be a sports statistician, Ms. Lee. I wanted to be a sports statistician because I liked to analyze sports with numbers. Above all, I love ice hockey. A sports statistician is someone who collects data and studies numbers about sports games. Our team is going to play against the Ice Lions this Saturday. Last week, I carefully watched the Ice Lions' games and analyzed their shots. I found that they let in 70 percent of the shots from the left side. This showed their left side was weak. Then, I shared this finding with our players. I'm happy to use my data to help our team.
① The Ice Lions let in 70 percent of shots from the right side.
② The Ice Lions' right side was found to be weak.
③ Ms. Lee shared her analysis with her own team's players.
④ Ms. Lee does not really like ice hockey.
⑤ Ms. Lee's team will play against the Ice Lions next month.

In this project, students designed, built, and raced electric cars. Six months ago, Ella and her team started with a simple car kit. They planned everything together, choosing the design, fixing the tires, and deciding where to place the motor and batteries. Ella said, “When I pressed the button, the car moved forward. The whole process made me so excited!” On race day, Ella was one of the drivers. “I was excited but a little nervous,” she said. Racing on the track was intense, but Ella loved it. “It felt fast and amazing — like flying on the ground!” Ella's favorite part was learning how things work. “I learned so much about engineering—how to build, fix problems, and work as a team.” She now dreams of becoming an engineer or a race car driver.
① Minji: Ella dreams of building and racing her own electric car.
② Sujin: She took part in the project to design, build, and race electric cars.
③ Yerin: She felt scared and quit racing once the race began.
④ Hyunwoo: She now hopes to work as an engineer or a race car driver.
