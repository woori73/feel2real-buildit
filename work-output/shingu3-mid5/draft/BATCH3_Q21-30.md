**30.** 윗글에서 추론할 수 있는 것으로 가장 적절한 것은?
> Zaha Hadid designed Dongdaemun Design Plaza. When it was first built, many people said it looked like a spaceship from the future. But Hadid said that her curved lines came from nature. Today, people from all over the world take pictures in front of the building, and it is one of the most famous places in Seoul.

① Hadid designed the plaza to look like a real spaceship.
② The building's popularity comes from its special design connected to nature.
③ People stopped visiting the plaza after learning about the design.
