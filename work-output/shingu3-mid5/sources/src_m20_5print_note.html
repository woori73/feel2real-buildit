문법 5-1 · A 가정법

1. 가정법 과거

If I studied hard, I could pass the exam.

2. 가정법 과거완료

If I had studied hard, I could have passed the exam.

3. 혼합가정

If I had studied hard, I could pass the exam.

★ Exercise

A 괄호 안에서 알맞은 말을 골라 문장을 완성해 봅시다.

1. If I (win, won) the lottery, I (will, would) give 100 dollars to you.
2. If she really (likes, had liked) him, she won't do such a horrible thing to him.
3. If I (drove, had driven) safely, I would (not be, not have been) in the hospital now.
4. If we (didn't set, hadn't set) the alarm, we would (have, been) late for the meeting.

[wish 가정법]

5. Ted is a nurse. He wishes he (were / had been) a doctor.

[wish 가정법]

6. Jack wasted his time at school; now he is sorry. He wishes he (studied / had studied) hard at school.
선생님 필기 · 가정법 핵심
① 가정법 과거: 현재 사실의 반대 → If + 주어 + 과거형, 주어 + would/could + 동사원형.
② 가정법 과거완료: 과거 사실의 반대 → If + 주어 + had p.p., 주어 + would/could have p.p.
③ 혼합가정: 과거의 반대 → 현재 결과 → If + had p.p., would/could + 동사원형. 사진 필기에서 “과거 → 지금”으로 강조됨.
Exercise 정답
1. won / would
2. likes
3. had driven / not be — 과거의 운전이 현재 병원에 있는 결과와 연결되는 혼합가정.
4. hadn’t set / have
5. were
6. had studied

문법 5-2 · B 주어진 문장과 의미가 같도록 가정법 문장을 써 봅시다.

1. As I don't have a problem, I don't call him for help.
→ If I had a problem, I would call him for help.
2. As I didn't move to Seoul last year, I couldn't meet his family more often.
→ If I had moved to Seoul last year, I could have met his family more often.
3. As I worked part-time after school, I could earn extra money.
→ If I hadn’t worked part-time after school, I couldn’t have earned extra money.
4. As phone rates are too expensive, I can't call my friend in America.
→ If phone rates weren’t too expensive, I could call my friend in America.
5. As I have enough time, I can help you with your homework.
→ If I didn’t have enough time, I couldn’t help you with your homework.
6. You didn't have breakfast - that's why you're hungry now.
→ If you had had breakfast, you wouldn’t be hungry now.

[wish 가정법]

7. I don't have a car. I want to have one.
→ I wish I had a car.
8. I have a cold. I don't like that I am sick.
→ I wish I didn’t have a cold. / I weren’t sick.
9. We are not on vacation now, but I want us to be on vacation now.
→ I wish we were on vacation now.
10. I regret that I complained to my mother about everything.
→ I wish I hadn’t complained to my mother about everything.
선생님 필기 포인트
현재 사실의 반대는 과거형, 과거 사실의 반대는 had p.p.를 사용. 6번은 과거의 아침 식사 여부가 현재의 배고픔에 영향을 주므로 혼합가정. wish도 현재 아쉬움은 과거형, 과거 후회는 had p.p.로 표현.

Reading I Text

From Trash to Music

Tears of joy are rolling down my cheeks. I am so happy and thrilled. If I were a bird, I would fly. I look around. The other members of my orchestra are hugging one another. Our concert has just finished, and everyone is standing and giving us a big hand. None of us ever expected that this day would come. It has been a long journey.

My name is Andrea and I'm a violinist in the Recycled Orchestra. Why is it called the Recycled Orchestra? It's because our musical instruments are made of objects from a landfill. That's why it's also known as the Landfill Harmonic Orchestra. Most of us in the orchestra are from Cateura, a small town in Paraguay. There is a huge landfill in our town. Some people even say that Cateura itself is a giant landfill. Many of us are poor. There weren't many hopes and dreams in our town. Everything began to change, however, when we met Favio Chávez.

Mr. Chávez was an environmental educator and a musician. He wanted to teach us music, but there was a big problem. There were only a few musical instruments in the whole town. We couldn't afford to buy new ones. But Mr. Chávez didn't give up. He said that we could make musical instruments with objects from the landfill. A talented man named Nicolás Gómez was able to put this idea into practice. He made cellos from oil drums. He turned water pipes into flutes.

We had another problem. No one knew how to play musical instruments. We didn't even know how to read music. Mr. Chávez taught us with great patience. Step by step, we began to make some sounds on our instruments. I still remember the first piece of music that we played. It was very short and mostly out of tune. But it was the most beautiful music to us. We felt a new hope in our hearts. From then on, we gathered to practice every day.

One day, Mr. Chávez told us some great news. We were going to have a concert, a real concert! And here we are now in front of hundreds of people. They love our music. The world sends us garbage, but we send back music!

Reading I · 선생님 필기 정리
1단락 — each other = one another(서로). If I were a bird, I would fly: 가정법 과거. has just finished / has been: 현재완료. everyone은 단수 취급. give us a big hand = 박수갈채를 보내다. None of us: 우리 중 아무도 ~않다. that this day would come에서 that은 명사절 접속사.
2단락 — be known as = ~로 알려지다. Cateura, a small town in Paraguay는 동격. even = 심지어. itself는 재귀대명사(강조 용법). when we met Favio Chávez: 그를 만났을 때부터 변화가 시작됨.
3단락 — teach + 사람 + 내용: teach us music. a few는 ‘몇몇의/조금 있는’ 긍정 의미. afford 뒤에는 to부정사. ones는 앞의 musical instruments를 대신함. be able to = ~할 수 있다. put ... into practice = 실행에 옮기다. turn A into B = A를 B로 바꾸다. made cellos from oil drums: 원재료의 형태·성질이 바뀌는 경우 made from.
4단락 — how to play / how to read: 의문사 + to부정사. with great patience = 큰 인내심을 가지고. step by step = 단계적으로. still = 여전히. the first piece of music that we played: 목적격 관계대명사. out of tune = 음정이 맞지 않는. the most beautiful music: 최상급.
5단락 — every day = 매일(부사구), everyday = 일상적인(형용사). From then on = 그때부터 계속. tell + 사람 + 내용: told us some great news. news는 형태는 -s지만 불가산 단수 취급. hundreds of = 수백의. The world sends us garbage, but we send back music!는 오케스트라의 motto로 표시.

If I were a bird, I would fly. — 내가 새라면, 나는 날아오를 텐데.
If she had a flying carpet, she could travel all over the world. — 그녀가 날아다니는 양탄자를 가지고 있다면, 그녀는 전 세계를 여행할 수 있을 텐데.
If he knew my address, he would send me a letter. — 그가 내 주소를 안다면, 그는 내게 편지를 보낼 텐데.

(1) 가정법 과거의 형태

If + 주어 + 동사의 과거형 ~, 주어 + 조동사의 과거형(would/could/should/might) + 동사원형 ...

(2) 가정법 과거의 의미와 쓰임

‘만약 ~한다면, ...할 텐데’의 뜻으로, 현재 사실과 반대되는 가정을 나타낼 때 사용한다.

※ if절에 쓰이는 be동사의 과거형은 were만 사용하지만, 구어체에서는 주어가 ‘I’ 또는 3인칭 단수인 경우 ‘was’를 쓰기도 한다.

[Check Up] A · 주어진 문장과 의미가 같도록 가정법 문장을 써 봅시다.

1. As he doesn’t have enough money, he can not buy the bike.
→ If he had enough money, he could buy the bike.
2. As I’m not you, I don’t call her.
→ If I were you, I would call her.
3. As I am not a doctor, I can’t help sick people.
→ If I were a doctor, I could help sick people.
4. As you are not with me, I can’t show you my painting.
→ If you were with me, I could show you my painting.

B · 주어진 우리말과 뜻이 같도록 가정법 문장을 써 봅시다.

1. 내가 요술램프를 가지고 있다면, 세 가지 소원을 빌 텐데.
→ If I had a magic lamp, I would wish three wishes.
2. 만약 내가 슈퍼맨이라면, 나는 위험에 처한 사람들을 구할 텐데.
→ If I were Superman, I would save people in danger.
3. 그가 피곤하지 않다면, 나와 함께 쇼핑을 갈 수 있을 텐데.
→ If he were not tired, he could go shopping with me.
4. 내가 타임머신이 있다면, 세종대왕을 만나러 갈 수 있을 텐데.
→ If I had a time machine, I would go to meet King Sejong.
5. 내가 한국의 대통령이라면, 우리나라를 더 아름답게 만들 텐데.
→ If I were president of Korea, I would make my country beautiful.
사진 필기 반영 · 추가
가정법 과거는 현재 사실의 반대를 나타내며, 사진의 were, could/would + 동사원형 적용 필기를 반영했습니다.

문법 5-4 · B 의문사 + to부정사 【추가 자료】

교과서 p.96 예문
We didn’t know how to read music. — 우리는 어떻게 악보를 읽는지 몰랐다.
I told him when to feed the dog. — 나는 언제 개에게 먹이를 줄지 그에게 말했다.
Alex found out where to buy the book. — Alex는 그 책을 어디에서 사야 할지 알아냈다.

(1) 형태와 의미: 의문사 + to + 동사원형

형태의미형태의미
when + to + 동사원형언제 ~할지how + to + 동사원형어떻게 ~할지, ~하는 방법
where + to + 동사원형어디서 ~할지what + to + 동사원형무엇을 ~할지
who(m) + to + 동사원형누구를/누구에게 ~할지which + to + 동사원형어느 것을 ~할지

(2) 쓰임과 역할

의문사절의 주어와 주절의 주어나 목적어가 같을 때 의문사절을 축약하여 ‘의문사 + to부정사’ 형태로 쓸 수 있다. ‘의문사 + to부정사’는 의문사 + 주어 + should + 동사원형으로 바꿔 쓸 수 있다.

How to start is the most important part of all. <주어>
She didn’t know where to go. <목적어>
My problem was how to contact everyone. <보어>

※ why + to부정사 불가능 → why I should ~ 절로 써야 함.

[Check Up] A · ‘의문사 + to부정사’를 사용해 바꿔 쓰세요.

1. They didn’t decide when they should leave for the station.
→ They didn’t decide when to leave for the station.
2. I can’t park here. Could you tell me where I should park?
→ Could you tell me where to park?
3. The manager explained to me how I should use the washing machine.
→ The manager explained to me how to use the washing machine.
4. She doesn’t know who she should listen to.
→ She doesn’t know who to listen to.

B · 괄호의 단어와 <보기>의 의문사를 이용해 완성하세요.

<보기> where · when · what · which · who · why

1. Have you decided where to go for your vacation? (go)
2. What should I do next? Dad, please tell me what to do. (do)
3. I like both of these T-shirts in this store. I’m not sure which to buy. (buy)
4. The students don’t understand why they should do their homework. (do)
사진 필기 반영 · 추가
① when/where/how/who + 주어 + should + 동사원형 → 의문사 + to + 동사원형으로 축약.
② 사진에서 why에는 X 표시가 되어 있습니다. why + to부정사는 불가하므로 4번은 why they should do처럼 절을 유지합니다.
③ 사진의 where to go / what to do / which to buy 필기를 반영했습니다.
