Lesson 6 · Language Use <so that>

A. 목적을 나타내는 so that

The architect took the curved lines from nature so that city people could enjoy them.
He spoke loudly so that everyone could hear him.
Emily went to bed early so that she could get up early.

so that + 주어 + 조동사 = “~하기 위해서, ~하도록”의 목적.
(so as) to + 동사원형 / in order to + 동사원형 / in order that + 주어 + can·may·will + 동사원형으로 바꿔 쓸 수 있음.

She jogs every morning so that she can stay healthy.
→ She jogs every morning in order that she can stay healthy.
→ She jogs every morning (so as) to / in order to stay healthy.

Check Up A

1. I set the alarm so that I wouldn’t be late. 내가 늦지 않도록
2. She practices hard in order to prepare for the contest. 그 대회를 준비하기 위해서
3. I went to bed early so that I could catch the first train. 내가 첫 기차를 타기 위해서

Check Up B · 어순

1. They whispered so that they could keep a secret.
2. I will draw a map so that you won’t get lost.

Check Up C · 어법

1. Open the window in order to fresh air can come in. → in order that fresh air can come in
2. Brian saved money so that he can buy a new smartphone. → could

Exercise

1. I turned off the radio so that I could concentrate on studying math.
2. We collected old clothes so that we could donate them.
3. He practices singing very hard so that he can be a singer.
4. Yumi swims every morning so that she can stay healthy.
5. Please speak louder so that everyone can hear you.

의미가 같도록 바꾸기

1. Yumi studies hard to pass the exam.
→ Yumi studies hard in order to pass the exam.
→ Yumi studies hard so that she can pass the exam.

2. I wrote his name in my diary not to forget it.
→ I wrote his name in my diary in order not to forget it.
→ I wrote his name in my diary so that I wouldn’t forget it.

3. My father went to the airport to pick up my sister.
→ My father went to the airport so as to pick up my sister.
→ My father went to the airport in order that he could pick up my sister.

Lesson 6 · Main Reading

Nature Meets City · 교과서 p.109–112

p.109 · Nature Meets City

Have you heard of the expression, “Art imitates nature”? Many artists get their ideas and inspirations from the world around them. This is because the natural world is a beautiful place. The shapes in nature are very pleasing to the eye. For example, look at the egg on the left. Isn’t it beautiful? It is round and delicate, yet strong enough to protect its contents. Can you imagine a building that looks like an egg? Such a building actually exists in London.

필기 핵심
  • yet: ‘그러나/그럼에도 불구하고’의 역접 연결.
  • the eye: 일반적인 사람의 눈/시각을 가리키는 표현.
  • For example: 예시를 드는 연결어.
  • It = the egg. round and delicate, yet strong enough to protect its contents 구조에 주의.
  • enough to + 동사원형: ‘~할 만큼 충분히 …한’.
  • actually exists: ‘실제로 존재한다’.

p.110 · The Sagrada Familia

Nature has inspired many architects around the world. This is the Sagrada Familia in Spain. It is one of the most famous churches in the world. Look at the beautiful tall columns inside the church. They look like trees, don’t they? The famous architect, Antoni Gaudi, used the shape of trees in the Sagrada Familia. That’s how he brought the beauty of nature indoors.

필기 핵심
  • It is one of the most famous churches in the world.: one of the + 최상급 + 복수명사.
  • They look like trees, don’t they?: 부가의문문. 긍정문 뒤에는 부정형 부가의문문.
  • used the shape of trees: 나무의 모양을 사용했다.
  • bring A to B: A를 B로 가져오다. brought the beauty of nature indoors = 자연의 아름다움을 실내로 가져왔다.

p.111 · The Sydney Opera House

In the first two examples, we can easily see what inspired the architect. But in the next example from Australia, this is not so obvious. Jørn Utzon, the architect of the Sydney Opera House, took a shape from nature and added his imagination. Can you guess what inspired him? Many people think that it is the waves in the ocean or a sailing boat. But interestingly, the inspiration came from an orange. Look at the roof closely. Can you see the peels of an orange? When orange lights are shone on the building, you can see the peels more clearly.

필기 핵심
  • this is not so obvious: 앞의 두 사례와 달리 무엇이 건축가에게 영감을 주었는지가 그렇게 분명하지 않다는 뜻.
  • the inspiration came from an orange: 영감은 오렌지에서 왔다.
  • 흔히 파도나 범선이라고 생각하지만 실제 본문 답은 an orange.

p.112 · Dongdaemun Design Plaza

What about Korea? Have you ever been to Dongdaemun Design Plaza in Seoul? Many people think that the building looks like a giant spaceship. But the architect, Zaha Hadid, took the curved lines from nature so that city people could enjoy them. Thanks to its special design, it has become a popular tourist attraction in Seoul. As you can see, many buildings try to capture the beauty of nature in their design. They are perfect examples of “Nature meets city.” If you were an architect, what would you choose from nature?

필기 핵심
  • Have you ever been to ~?: ‘~에 가 본 적이 있니?’ 현재완료 경험.
  • took the curved lines from nature: 자연에서 곡선을 가져왔다.
  • so that city people could enjoy them: 목적의 so that, ‘도시 사람들이 그것들을 즐길 수 있도록’.
  • Thanks to: ‘~ 덕분에’.
  • capture the beauty of nature: 자연의 아름다움을 담아내다.
  • If you were an architect, what would you choose from nature?: 가정법 과거 형태.
