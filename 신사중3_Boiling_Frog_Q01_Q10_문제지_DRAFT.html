(가) Humans are good at ______ gradual changes.
(나) The frog becomes ______ the rising temperature.
(다) We may become ______ changes that once seemed unusual.
① adapt to / accustomed to / oblivious to
② adapting to / accustomed to / oblivious to
③ adapt / accustomed / oblivious
④ adapting / accustomed / oblivious
⑤ adapt to / accustomed / oblivious to

3.다음 개념 설명(출제용 재구성)을 읽고, <보기>의 ㄱ~ㄹ 중 옳은 것만을 모두 묶은 것은? (3점)
Myth: A story that is believed or told as if it were true, but may not be scientifically accurate.
Metaphor: A comparison used to explain an idea.
The frog story is a myth, but the metaphor can still be useful.
<보기>
ㄱ. Myth는 과학적으로 검증된 사실이다.
ㄴ. Metaphor는 아이디어를 설명하기 위한 비교이다.
ㄷ. Boiling frog story는 myth이자 metaphor이다.
ㄹ. Myth와 metaphor는 본질적으로 같은 의미이다.
① ㄱ, ㄴ ② ㄱ, ㄷ ③ ㄴ, ㄷ ④ ㄴ, ㄹ ⑤ ㄷ, ㄹ
4.다음 빈칸 (A)~(E)에 들어갈 말로 가장 적절한 짝은? (4점)
(A) Scientists have identified 1.5°C of warming above pre-industrial levels as an important ______.
(B) The ______ risks are serious, but we can still take action.
(C) Small changes can ______ over time and create serious problems.
(D) These gases ______ heat, keeping it in the atmosphere instead of letting it escape into space.
(E) We need to ______ our carbon footprints by using less energy.
① (A) threshold (B) impending (C) accumulate (D) trap (E) reduce
② (A) threshold (B) impending (C) exacerbate (D) release (E) offset
③ (A) consequence (B) catastrophic (C) accumulate (D) trap (E) mitigate
④ (A) threshold (B) significant (C) perceive (D) trap (E) reduce
⑤ (A) threshold (B) impending (C) accumulate (D) release (E) offset
5.다음 도식의 순서와 내용에 맞게 대화 ⓐ~ⓒ를 완성하려고 한다. <보기>에서 골라 바르게 짝지은 것은? (4점)

A: Then what happens?

② ⓐ-②, ⓑ-①, ⓒ-③

6.다음 세 문장에서 빈칸 뒤의 내용을 사실로 단정하지 않고 '만약 ~한다면'이라는 가정적 조건으로 나타내려고 한다. 빈칸에 공통으로 들어갈 접속사로 가장 적절한 것은? (3점)
(가) ______ we burn fossil fuels, we release carbon dioxide into the atmosphere.
(나) ______ greenhouse gas emissions increase, more heat stays in the atmosphere.
(다) ______ we continue at the current rate, these activities could exacerbate climate change.
① Since ② If ③ Although ④ However ⑤ Like
7.다음 글의 ⓐ~ⓔ 중, 어법상 틀린 것을 모두 고르면? (정답 2개, 4점)
ⓐ Much of modern life depend on fossil fuels such as coal and oil.
ⓑ When we burn them, carbon dioxide is released into the atmosphere.
ⓒ These gases trap heat that would otherwise escape into space.
ⓓ As emissions increase, more heat stay in the atmosphere.
ⓔ Our everyday choices contribute to this problem through our carbon footprints.
① ⓐ ② ⓑ ③ ⓒ ④ ⓓ ⑤ ⓔ
8.조동사 수동태(조동사 + be + p.p.) 규칙을 적용하여, 다음 문장 중 어법상 옳은 것을 두 개 고르시오. (4점)
① The threshold must be identify by scientists.
② Climate change can be mitigate if we act now.
③ Emissions should be reduced immediately.
④ The damage could be avoided with early action.
⑤ We must be act before it's too late.
9.다음 단어와 영영풀이가 일치하지 않는 것은? (3점)
① accumulate: to gather or increase gradually over time
② insidious: happening gradually but having harmful effects
③ mitigate: to make something less severe or serious
④ threshold: the level at which something starts to happen
⑤ impending: already happened and finished
10.본문에서 밑줄 친 세 표현의 의미가 문맥상 올바르게 짝지어진 것을 모두 고르면? (정답 2개, 4점)
"This makes climate change an insidious problem." (문단 2)
"Scientists have identified 1.5°C of warming above pre-industrial levels as an important threshold. Beyond this point, many negative effects of climate change are expected to become increasingly severe." (문단 4)
"More extreme heat, rising sea levels, and damage to ecosystems could become harder to mitigate." (문단 4)
① insidious problem → 쉽게 눈에 띄는 문제
② insidious problem → 서서히 진행되어 알아채기 힘든 문제
③ threshold → 그 수준을 넘어서면 기후변화의 여러 부정적 영향이 더 심해질 것으로 예상되는 기준점
④ threshold → 시작점에 불과한 낮은 기준
⑤ mitigate → 악화시키다
⑥ mitigate → 임박한 위험을 피하다
